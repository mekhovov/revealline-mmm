## Effective prompt

Create a beautiful professional art-direction comparison board for a modern pixel-art territory-reveal arcade game. Wide landscape image, four equally important framed gameplay concept panels in a clean 2 by 2 layout on a restrained warm off-white gallery background, generous gutters. Typography only four short titles, neatly typeset: FPV FRONT, UKRAINE ATLAS, 1994 FOREVER, NAVI NETWORK. Each panel shows a flat top-down rectangular arena with exactly the same simple compositional idea: a muted dark unrevealed region at the upper right, a large L-shaped revealed region along the left and bottom, one thin bright active right-angle trail extending from safety into the dark area, one tiny player at its endpoint, two distinct bright outlined live enemy tokens floating in the dark area. The deliberate gameplay overlay is always crisp, readable pixel art, while the art revealed beneath can use a different medium. These are conceptual visual studies, no device frames, no code, no busy dashboard, no invented small text.

Panel 1 FPV FRONT, main direction: luxurious 1990s Amiga-style pixel art, Ukrainian farmland at golden dawn, wheat gold, olive, slate blue, tiny fictional invading Russian military trucks and armored vehicles discoverable in the revealed picture; a small blue-and-yellow Ukrainian quadcopter avatar at the trail endpoint, ivory-gold trail, warm red angular hostile drone tokens inside dark fog. Show military machinery as part of the revealed image, distinct from active enemy tokens. Non-graphic atmosphere. A tiny restrained Ukrainian stitch motif on one frame corner.

Panel 2 UKRAINE ATLAS: the revealed picture is beautiful watercolor on cream paper, a Carpathian hillside, wooden village rooftops, fir forests, soft jade and honey colors, subtle watery pigment texture. The active player is a tiny crisp golden pixel bird, the active path is high-contrast deep blue edged with cream. Quiet cream and moss frame with a restrained geometric weave detail. The watercolor image stays painterly, and only the live gameplay layer is pixel art.

Panel 3 1994 FOREVER: rich original pixel artwork of an after-school 1990s computer room, beige CRT computer, floppy disks, warm sunset through the window, small houseplants, plum shadows, cyan and coral accents. Not a wall of neon. A tiny pixel star cursor leaves a cyan line; threats have distinct amber zigzag silhouettes. Frame suggests a subtle old desktop program bezel, with no readable interface text beyond the title.

Panel 4 NAVI NETWORK: beautifully simple smooth illustrated miniature business city in Coupa-inspired blue, white, turquoise and warm yellow, supplier buildings and connected street plazas gradually revealed. One clean original blue pixel C-token as the temporary player, a turquoise path, small amber leak symbols as clearly separate active hazards. Friendly polished business arcade visual, sparse pixel coin sparkles on captured areas. This is a concept token, not a claim to reproduce an official mascot design.

Unify composition, readable gameplay silhouettes and confident art direction, while clearly preserving four different worlds and at least two different artwork media. Handcrafted aesthetic, clean hierarchy, rich but controlled detail. Do not add photorealistic room mockups, gradients across pixel sprites, soft blurred active paths, excessive bloom, giant percentages or extra branding.
