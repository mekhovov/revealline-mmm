## Visual review

The generator added incidental city signage beyond the requested labels, and the percentage readouts are illustrative. The Coupa capture path is not a validated example of the intended region-fill algorithm. The Ukrainian borders are more ornate than we would use around a small phone board. Future iterations should test a thinner frame, landscape and portrait layouts, real sprite scale, and Ukrainian-language typography. This board must not be treated as a ready-to-slice sprite atlas; final assets need consistent grids, transparent layers, animation frames and in-game testing.
