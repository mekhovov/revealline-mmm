# Campaign, input and reward follow-up

Research checked 2026-09-13. This note informs the next source milestone; it does not change v0.35 or claim that unreleased chapters are installed in the public game.

## Reference evidence and decisions

The [official XPOSED Reloaded listing](https://store.playstation.com/en-ca/product/UP2538-CUSA31959_00-XRFULLUS00000001) describes territory capture, revealed backgrounds, unlocked packs and demanding challenges with simple controls. These support keeping the picture reward, short retry loop and understandable inputs central. The listing does not establish precise turning, button-release or hit-propagation timing. Preserve the earlier manual/footage evidence classifications; no new video or soundtrack listening pass was performed for this note.

[Xbox Accessibility Guideline 107](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/107) recommends digital as well as analog operation throughout the interface, single non-simultaneous presses, and alternatives to sustained holds. Apply that to chapter installation, selection, recovery, story controls and Collection as well as the arena. The current tap-to-steer and explicit Resume contracts remain appropriate. A keyboard-accessible title screen alone is not complete input qualification.

The [Game Accessibility Guidelines](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/full-list/) recommend consistent input across gameplay and menus, restrained menu depth and recognizable saved-game information. The concrete follow-up is a complete journey matrix: title → chapter → briefing → flight → pause/load → win/story → Collection. Keep Install separate from Choose: downloading content must not replace the player's unfinished flight. Show the exact chapter, picture and progress that Continue will restore.

The [Screen Wake Lock API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Screen_Wake_Lock_API) can prevent an active mobile session from dimming, but the browser may release the lock and support is conditional. A later device-polish feature can offer “Keep screen awake while playing,” acquire only for a visible active game, release when no longer needed, and treat refusal as an ordinary fallback. This is a proposed convenience, not implemented or device-certified behavior.

## Next production choices

1. **Finish portable optional chapters.** Move exact original image bytes into the existing managed media library while retaining small, validated chapter recipes. Keep space limits, explicit installation, saved/earned identity and rollback behavior. Verify actual game backup hooks before calling metadata export a complete backup.
2. **Adopt nine new themed rewards.** Ukraine, retro and spend-management Route Worlds use nine original pictures over three existing route geometries. They are theme variants, not nine new layouts. Their source compiler and ordinary-input proofs are complete; public catalog adoption remains separate.
3. **Build three genuinely new Sentinel Circuit missions.** Scout information, a timed carrier control field, and a fiber approach with a warned boss opening each introduce one decision. The final encounter uses the registered two-schedule boss; the three missions are not three internal boss phases. Keep an alternative route and a missed-opening recovery path.
4. **Make objectives and unlocks readable.** Explain the mandatory objective separately from optional clean/speed mastery. Give each role its effect, risk and counterplay in the briefing. Do not require equipment merely because a button exists.
5. **Complete the quality gates after integration.** Exact-source tests and replay reconstruction establish solvability and consistency. Native browser journeys establish observable behavior. Human challenge, physical touch/controller comfort, animation readability and soundtrack listening remain distinct work.

The spend-management artwork uses fictional procurement, reuse, supply and information motifs informed by [Coupa's business spend management overview](https://www.coupa.com/blog/how-business-spend-management-can-help-accelerate-financial-transformation/) and [community-driven AI discussion](https://www.coupa.com/blog/an-8-trillion-advantage-coupas-community-driven-ai-turns-transactions-into-intelligent-insights/). It does not reproduce a real Coupa interface, mascot or financial outcome. Full image prompts, original bytes and provenance are retained in the three artwork libraries.

## Integration order

The next source branch first brings together the source-qualified chapter recipes and artwork. The external chapter host and its game-data backup adapters then receive their own integration checks. Only after native install/choose/save/load/reward/restore passes should new entries appear in the public catalog. Keep generated files, raw evidence and original artwork separately identified. Publish a new frozen release through a reviewed PR and verify its live Pages content; never alter an existing release in place.
