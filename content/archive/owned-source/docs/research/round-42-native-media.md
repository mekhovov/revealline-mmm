[MDN's storage guidance](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Storage_API/Storage_quotas_and_eviction_criteria) documents that quota generally applies to an origin, not each URL path; storage estimates are approximate; writes can fail with `QuotaExceededError`; and best-effort data can be evicted. Requesting persistence is separate from having it granted. Accordingly, an installed pack is available without another download **while its stored bytes remain available**. A successful offline test is not a guarantee of permanent retention. Keep explicit original-byte exports and transactional refusal/recovery. These are implementation decisions informed by the documentation, not new browser measurements.
