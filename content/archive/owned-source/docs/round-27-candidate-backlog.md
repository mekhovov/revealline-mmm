Status: proposal only, researched on 2026-09-12 while First Flight v0.16.0 is under browser review. No artwork was generated, pack changed or new release verified for this proposal.

This directly improves the reward for completing a mission and broadens the four-theme direction without adding another copy of an existing map. It is a bounded content increment, not a claim that six pictures fulfill the user's complete-art ambition.

## Three original picture briefs

Use the existing Workshop level names as picture titles; gallery titles currently come from the level, not image metadata. Preserve the existing three music recipes and seven-class roster. The common prompt plus one scene brief below forms the complete initial generation prompt. Record subsequent edits in full, not just the template name.

**Common prompt:** “Create one original full-bleed 4:3 collectible background for a modern pixel-art territory-capture game. Prefer 1536×1152 output; actual file dimensions will be checked. Coherent visible pixel clusters, crisp silhouettes, selective dithering, restrained but luminous colors and a memorable central subject that remains readable at a 320-pixel-wide board. Design foreground, middle distance and background with rewarding small discoveries and quieter passages so a bright live cut stays readable during partial reveal. No UI, text, logos, score numbers, game markers, arrows, frame, collage, active player character or enemies. No screenshots, copied compositions or recognizable commercial game characters. Painted illumination may be rich; do not fake animation frames or promise interactive objects. Produce a finished single picture, not a concept sheet.”

## Separate small save-usability candidate

Round26 source browser review also reproduced a pre-existing restriction: at normal Ready, a saved flight can be listed and loaded, but **Export attempt** invokes the current-flight `suspend` callback and reports “Start an unfinished campaign flight to save it.” **Export complete backup** correctly includes the existing saved slot. This behavior was present before First Flight and does not invalidate its checked handoff.

A later bounded fix could distinguish exporting the current unfinished flight from exporting a validated stored attempt when Ready. Preserve the original slot, require a valid bounded session envelope, report an absent/unreadable slot, and never start or overwrite an attempt merely to export it. Check real Ready/current-flight/recovery/terminal cases and cancelled downloads through the public controls. Keep this separate from the original-picture deliverable; do not describe individual Ready export as working until implemented and verified.
