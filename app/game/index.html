Playing a downloaded copy?

Open the folder containing game in a terminal, then start a local web server:

python3 -m http.server 8768 --bind 127.0.0.1

Leave that terminal open and visit 127.0.0.1:8768/game/. If that port is busy, choose a free port in both the command and address.

Opening game/index.html directly as a file cannot start the game. Python is only needed for this local-server option. Playing online opens a different site; local saves are not transferred automatically.

      THE SHORT VERSION

      Make the next cut count.

      Read without starting or resuming.

      1. Blue ground is safe from field enemies. Leaving it begins a vulnerable gold line.
      2. Return to blue ground to close the cut. Regions without a field enemy are revealed. In the revised Arcade chapters, closing a cut stops your craft: tap a fresh direction to fly again.
      3. Enemies on both sides can leave only your line captured. Use that line to begin another cut.
      4. A field enemy touching your line, or crossing your own line, costs a life. Border patrols can also hit you on the outer edge.
      5. Reach the coverage target and capture required objectives. Special challenge maps can limit the clock or live-line length; their briefing and HUD explain those limits.

      Keyboard: arrows/WASD, Shift boost, E ability, R supply, Escape/P pause. Touch: drag the floating stick or swipe to turn. Choose a sliding D-pad, either hand, size and opacity in Settings. Releasing keeps you moving. Pause to take a break.

      Controller: Release controls and press a face button to join. Configure the layout in Settings.

      Text entry and file pickers still use keyboard or touch. Some browsers also need a keyboard or touch action before sound can start. Menu navigation and silent gameplay remain available.

      Each class explains its actual effect in the flight deck. Scout shows current direction hints and hidden objectives; supplies refill charge-based classes. A shield absorbs one contact by safely cancelling the line.

      Campaign difficulty: Standard and Gentle reveal the same pictures and share mission and appearance unlocks. Scores and medals stay separate; equipment seals require Standard. A change during a flight applies to the next fresh attempt. Resume and Load preserve the current flight; Retry starts the selected difficulty from the beginning.

      Long flights: Replay recording and recoverable saves stop after 30 minutes of play. A flight can continue, but a completion after two hours cannot be kept in the collection. Pause when taking a break, and export your library to keep a backup.

        Keyboard controls

        Choose a preset or change any action. Keys use physical positions, so W means the W position on a US keyboard. Escape always pauses; Escape or Tab cancels key capture. Keyboard changes affect solo play and its practice preview. Couch players keep their separate controls.

        This setting is separate from the button-map draft. Select Hold to reset it.

        Touch & on-screen steering

        Auto shows steering during touch play. Keyboard or controller use hides it. Choose Always to steer with a mouse. Drag anywhere on the board, or use the thumb area below.

        A short drag turns immediately. Lift your finger to keep flying; use Pause to take a break. The stick starts at your first touch. Controls stay within thumb reach. In short landscape layouts, the D-pad reserves room beside the field.
