Field details

    YOUR FIRST CONNECTION

    1. Start small. Draw a short loop back to safe ground to claim it. Banking stops your craft; steer again for your next cut.
    2. Join when ready. Steer both moving heads together to bank a shared cut. Crossing an old part of a partner’s line is harmless.
    3. Watch the threats. Preparing the selected arena’s threat guidance.
    4. Cover each other. Preparing Support and rescue guidance.
    5. Secure the relay cores. Preparing relay objective guidance.

    Captures recharge both players’ Support and can rescue a downed partner.

    Both halves are contested. Create safe routes in small steps, then coordinate a larger cut.

    Tap a direction to keep moving. Banking a cut stops your craft; steer again to leave. Esc / P or Menu pauses both players. Resume together is always explicit.

    Sunflower: WASD steer, Left Shift Boost, Q Support.

    Skyline: arrow keys steer, Right Shift Boost, Enter Support.

    Controllers: stick or D-pad steers, right shoulder holds Boost, South uses Support, and Menu pauses. Each player keeps their controller assignment.
